# Greed Machine concept film

The user's direction is to explain the project idea and how it is supposed to work. The film uses intended mechanics throughout. Testnet rehearsal figures and receipts are excluded from the composition.

| Time | Idea | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–9s | A memecoin with mechanics; buy and hold | Olive opener, existing swarm artwork, holder journey |
| 9–18s | Fund an autonomous treasury | Launch proceeds, project trading fees, retained earnings |
| 18–27s | An AI swarm discovers and debates | Scout, strategist, challenger; one candidate |
| 27–36s | Security gates capital | Independent review, hard limits, reserves, emergency pause; agents have no treasury keys |
| 36–45s | Strategies work inside those rules | Allocate, monitor, exit, reconcile costs and losses |
| 45–54s | Share eligible net profit | Target60–70% to holders in stablecoins, remainder retained |
| 54–60s | Buy. Hold. Inspect. | Project idea and repository URL held through the end |

Keep the60-second landscape format, captions and the user's local KittenTTS2 voice preference. The product narrative is grounded in `PRODUCT.md` and `docs/business-model.md`.
