Greed Machine is a memecoin-treasury prototype that lets holders inspect decisions, costs, losses and payments. The app demonstrates competing proposals, independent review, fixed spending limits, loss recovery and protected failed-payment retries in a reproducible simulation. A separate completed NEAR testnet rehearsal moves actual demonstration tokens through funding, an operating expense, a capped vault allocation, settlement and frozen holder payments. NEAR enforces reserves, receiver restrictions and replay protection. Finalized evidence reconciles 1,004.55 gUSD in the treasury, 8.45 gUSD paid and zero unpaid liabilities. The walkthrough shows the app and read-only verification of those deployed contracts. gUSD is unbacked; fee income and vault returns are sponsor-funded fixtures. This does not demonstrate organic revenue, market yield or continuous model-to-onchain execution. The intended journey is buy GREED, hold and receive eligible stablecoin profits; launchpad fee routing, live DeFi and production USDC payouts are future work. Models never receive treasury keys. The public repository includes an app-only judge guide, tests, contracts and receipts.
