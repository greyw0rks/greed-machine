# Greed Machine

**A memecoin built around a business.**

Greed Machine is a memecoin project with a shared treasury: a pool of money used to run the business. The idea is simple: buy GREED, hold it, and receive a share of what the business earns.

A group of AI agents looks for opportunities, debates which ones are worth pursuing, and works within rules that protect the treasury. The goal is to share profits with holders while keeping enough money in the business to support its growth.

## What it does

Greed Machine brings the business into public view. Holders can see what the agents propose, why a decision was made, where money goes, and how the results are shared. Losses and costs stay visible too.

The intended holder experience is buy and hold. Ordinary payouts would arrive in a stablecoin such as USDC, without holders having to manage strategies or claim each payment. Holder votes are reserved for changes to the project's core rules.

## How it works

1. **Fund the business.** Part of the token launch proceeds and project trading fees would go into the treasury.
2. **Find opportunities.** AI agents propose ways to put that money to work and challenge each other's ideas.
3. **Check the risks.** A separate safety review and fixed spending rules decide whether an opportunity can receive funding. The business can keep its money in reserve when no proposal passes.
4. **Follow the results.** Approved activity is tracked. Costs and earlier losses must be covered before any new profit can be shared.
5. **Share and grow.** The planned holder share is 60–70% of the remaining profit. The rest stays in the treasury. The exact split is still being decided.

## Where it is today

The working demo shows the agents' discussions, how spending is controlled, and how failed payments can be completed later. A separate demonstration on NEAR has completed funding, an expense, an investment cycle and holder payments using demo tokens. NEAR applies the spending rules and records those payments.

The demo tokens have no cash backing, and the example investment returns are funded for the demonstration. The token launch, live investing and automatic USDC payouts are still being built.
